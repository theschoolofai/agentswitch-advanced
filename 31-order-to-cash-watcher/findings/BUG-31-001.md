# BUG-31-001: Invoice and applied-receipt read tools are not available to seat 31

Seat 31 bug report in the course format (`BUG_REPORT_FORMAT.md`). No passwords, tokens, customer data or platform detail: exact requests and replies are sent to the course team as text.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | Invoice and applied-receipt read tools are not available to seat 31 |
| Seat and login | 31, `team31a` |
| System | Suryodaya and Keystone |
| Date and time (IST) | First seen 6 Nov 2026, about 11:20 pm. Checked again 7 Nov 2026 at 00:11 am and later that morning; same result each time |
| Severity | Medium: blocks a first-task section; no data was changed |
| Repeats | Every time, on both systems |
| Status | Closed: fixed by the instructor, verified by us on 11 Nov |

**Steps** (the exact tool names and request text are in the version sent to the course team)
1. Log in as `team31a`.
2. Call the invoice list tool.
3. Call the applied-receipt list tool the same way.
4. List our tools, and search the tools for "invoice".
5. Open the linked-records view of an order that has an invoice.

**Expected**
The 3 Nov access update gives seat 31 the accounting tools. The instructor's 7 Nov answer says to read due date and balance from invoices, and to use the applied-receipt records for receipts. So both list tools should return rows.

**Actual**
- Steps 2–3, both systems, reply with error `tool_not_available`:
  > This tool is not available to your seat; it is not in your tools/list
- Step 4: our tool list has no invoice or applied-receipt tools on either system, and the search finds only the linked-records tool.
- Step 5: the invoice comes back redacted. Some other linked record types are redacted too.

**Evidence**
The exact request and reply text is sent to the course team as text. Nothing is attached here, so no platform data is included.

**Impact**
The "invoices overdue" section of the 15 Nov report is blocked, and so is matching the unallocated Suryodaya payments to invoices. Suite tasks 5–6 (`overdue_invoices`, `overdue_ageing`) depend on it. The rule is already written (commit `94e14fd`) and switches on as soon as the invoice tool is in our tool list, so no code change is needed on our side.

**Ask**
Please give `team31a` read access to the invoice and applied-receipt list and get tools on both systems. If seat 31 isn't meant to have them, please tell us how to read invoice due dates and balances instead.

**Log**
- 6 Nov, ~11:20 pm IST: found during the first exploration. Invoices linked from orders are redacted; no invoice tool.
- 7 Nov, 00:11 IST: after the instructor's answer, tried both list tools directly and was told they are not available, on both systems.
- 7 Nov, later: tool list checked again, still none. Posted in the seat group for the admin.
- 10 Nov, instructor (seat group): confirmed and fixed in code. The next release lets our login read invoices and received payments (read only); the instructor will post when it is live, and asked us not to wait for it to design the report.
- 11 Nov (read only): with a token from 6 Nov the read tools were still missing. After logging in again (about 12:48 pm IST), the invoice and received-payment list tools are in our tool list and return rows on both systems. The overdue-invoice section now switches on by itself.
- 12 Nov (read only): full read of both record types on both systems. Every invoice the report uses has the fields the instructor named on 7 Nov, and every applied receipt is paid and allocated to exactly one invoice, as the instructor said. Our own check of the receipts' allocation history counted each receipt twice (it added the receipt's posting event to its allocation event); fixed on our side, no platform change needed. The bug stays closed.

**Resolution:** read access granted by the instructor; verified by us on 11 Nov on both systems. Closed.
