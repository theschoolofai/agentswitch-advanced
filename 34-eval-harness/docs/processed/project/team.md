## Where we talk

- **Never** post passwords or tokens anywhere.

## Weekly rhythm (proposal)
| When | What |
|---|---|
| Monday | Pick this week's cards from the [board](board.md). At most one card in Doing per person. |
| During the week | Small pull requests. End each work session with an entry in [status.md](status.md). |
| Sunday | **Weekly update in the seat group** (a course rule): what we finished, what is next, one blocker. Review [risks.md](risks.md) together. |

Course rule: a week with no commits and no chat can cost the seat its place. Each of us makes at least one commit or chat message a week.

## Reviews
- **Our own pull requests.** Each one needs a review from a teammate on seat 34, and then the seat lead merges it.
- **The seat lead's pull requests.** A teammate reviews them, and the course team or another seat's lead merges them.
- **Nobody merges their own pull request.**
- **Other seats' pull requests.** The course also lets us review these. It is a good way to learn their agents before we write suites for them.
